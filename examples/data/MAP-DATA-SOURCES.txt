CRYChart geographical example data

These geographical example assets come from the independent sources below. Original download URLs and SHA-256 hashes, output hashes, and
transformations are recorded in map-data-provenance.json. prepare-maps.py is the
reproducible extraction script. All example data is embedded for offline use.

Natural Earth
  world-countries.json and us-contiguous-states.json
  Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
  Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
  Natural Earth vector and raster data are in the public domain. All polygon
  rings are retained; this project only reduces metadata and coordinate precision.

World Bank, World Development Indicators
  world-bank-population.json
  Indicators: SP.POP.TOTL (population total), SP.POP.GROW (annual growth %)
  Source: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/
  Terms: https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use
  License: CC BY 4.0 with the World Bank's additional terms and mandatory source
  acknowledgment passed to recipients. The downloaded indicator metadata did not
  indicate a different data license. Data revisions may differ from the 2018
  values displayed in the historical reference chart.

EnergieID entsoe-py geographical data
  nordic-bidding-zones.json
  https://github.com/EnergieID/entsoe-py/tree/master/entsoe/geo
  Repository copyright: 2017 EnergieID cvba-so; MIT notice retained separately in
  entsoe-MIT-LICENSE.txt. Only GeoJSON factual assets and license/source metadata
  were used, not plotting or library implementation code.
  Upstream source notes: Natural Earth for country geometries; Norway's NVE
  https://temakart.nve.no/link/?link=vannkraft for Norwegian zones; Swedish zone
  boundaries manually redrawn upstream from https://www.natomraden.se/.
  This is the upstream best-effort geographic layer, not an authoritative grid
  boundary determination. Energy generation/price profiles in example 1714 are
  independently generated and identified as such; they are not Nord Pool data.

Our World in Data COVID-19 historical dataset
  covid-history.json
  https://github.com/owid/covid-19-data/tree/master/public/data
  Mathieu, E., Ritchie, H., Ortiz-Ospina, E. et al. A global database of COVID-19
  vaccinations. Nature Human Behaviour (2021).
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-021-01122-8
  OWID-produced data is CC BY 4.0. The upstream repository states that third-party
  data retains its original authors' terms and supplies source documentation:
  https://github.com/owid/covid-19-data/blob/master/public/data/README.md
  Missing metrics remain null. The candidate uses recorded daily vaccination
  rates and seven-day-smoothed cases per million, not estimates of missing
  observations. Current historical revisions need not match a prior screenshot.

Dublin City Council / Google Project Air View
  dublin-air-view-route.json and dublin-road-segments.json
  Data attribution: Aclima & Google 2022 via Dublinked.
  https://data.gov.ie/dataset/google-airview-data-dublin-city
  Publisher declares Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Measurement timestamps are UTC; coordinates are GPS longitude/latitude and
  PM25_ugm3 is measured PM2.5 in micrograms per cubic metre. Extraction dates and
  bounds are recorded in the provenance manifest. Exposure arithmetic skips gaps
  between observations. The example is a chart demo, not an exposure assessment.
  The road dataset identifies OpenStreetMap osm_id/fclass/name fields. Its street
  geometry remains attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors and is distributed
  as a separate derived geometry asset under ODbL 1.0:
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  No OpenStreetMap raster tiles are downloaded or redistributed.

All CC BY assets include the attribution above. Changes consist of documented
selection, rounding, field removal and JSON repackaging, not new measurements.
